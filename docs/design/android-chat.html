What 2026 is doing, and what we take from it

Eight decisions from this year’s best work

Each card names a pattern that the strongest apps of 2025–26 share, then the decision it leads to here. Sources are listed at the end.

Google · Material 3 Expressive

Springs and shapes, with restraint

Android now moves on spring physics and morphing shapes. Google’s own rule is “expressive by default, restrained when necessary”.

Springs move the cards. A morphing shape shows the accountant is working. The only theatrical moment is the stamp.

Gemini · Neural Expressive, May 2026

The composer floats

Gemini’s redesign lifted the input into a floating pill. Reviewers liked the look but missed the suggestion chips it removed.

A floating pill composer, with chips kept for the next likely step.

OpenAI · Apps in ChatGPT guidelines

Cards before words

Inline UI appears before the model’s text, and only when it makes the task faster.

Every reply opens with its block (figure, voucher, table, file), then one short sentence. No carousels for money.

Agentic UX · Smashing, Feb 2026

Plan, confirm, receipt, undo

Agent launches this year all pause before spending or deleting, show what changed, and let you take it back.

Reading is free. Posting is a voucher you confirm, then a stamped receipt with a two-minute undo.

Apple · Liquid Glass

Glass only where it floats

The guidance keeps a clear line between content and controls, and warns against glass over scrolling lists.

Only the composer, the voice pill and sheets are translucent. Cards stay solid so figures stay legible.

“Dark mode 2.0”

Dark is designed, not inverted

Teams now design a separate dark palette with its own contrast, rather than flipping the light one.

A lapis night with lifted turquoise and warmer saffron, tuned for OLED and checked for contrast.

Copilot Money · finance UX

Calm money, fresh numbers

The best-reviewed finance apps feel calm, not clinical, and say how fresh every number is.

Every figure carries a freshness dot (“به‌روز ۹:۳۰”) and a comparison. Totals open to show their lines.

Apple Design Awards 2026

Charts are craft

This year’s Visuals and Graphics app winner is a tide chart. A clear chart still wins on its own.

Chart blocks drawn to scale, with an area fill, a faint grid and the latest point marked.

The screens

The same chat for a family, a company and a UK sole trader

There’s one layout throughout. What changes is the books badge, the blocks in the replies, and the theme the person picked.

The system

Persian tilework colours, a ledger’s typography

The palette extends the web app’s turquoise, saffron and navy ink into the colours of Iranian tiles: firouzeh turquoise, lapis, saffron and pomegranate. Each colour has one job.

Rules the design keeps

Six rules for every screen

The books are always named

Badge, card and seal carry the books’ name and colour. After a switch, the first posting asks once more.

Numbers in full where money moves

Vouchers show the full rial amount in tabular figures. Short forms like “۱٫۲ میلیارد” appear only in figures, and tap to the exact sum.

One accent per job

Turquoise means you can act. Saffron is personal books and attention. Pomegranate is money leaving or something late. Nothing else is coloured.

Motion means something happened

Springs follow your finger. The stamp marks a posting. Only listening and thinking animate on their own.

Persian first

The layout mirrors, the conversation runs right to left, digits are Persian, dates are Jalali. English screens switch every one of these.

Private on the lock screen

Notifications never show amounts unless you turn that on. The app locks with a fingerprint or PIN.
